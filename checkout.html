What We Deliver

Four outputs.
One corpus build.

01 ——

Your Searchable Archive

A private website built from your sermons. Search by passage, topic, illustration type, or doctrine. Find the story you told about forgiveness in 2019. Pull every time you've preached from Romans. Retrieve the illustration you need for Sunday — in seconds. Your life's work, finally findable.

02 ——

Your Voice Report

A data-driven analysis of how you preach. What doctrines dominate your corpus. Where your illustration repertoire is rich and where it's thin. How your rhetorical register compares to the preachers in our Guild Hall. Not a critique — a mirror. Most pastors have never seen their preaching from the outside. This is that view.

03 ——

Your Illustration Almanac

Hundreds of illustrations from your archive, extracted and organized by type and topic. Personal stories. Historical examples. Analogies. Metaphors. More of your material than you knew you had — and now organized so you can actually find and use it.

04 ——

Your Book Recommendation

Most pastors have already written a book — they just can't see it yet. We identify the latent book inside your archive: the topic you've treated most deeply, most consistently, and with the most compelling material. We tell you what it is and show you what's already in there.
